Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clawleaf collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you or the businesses we serve use our AI voice and messaging agent services, including over the WhatsApp Business Platform.

1. Who We Are

Clawleaf ("Clawleaf", "we", "us", or "our") is a healthcare-focused AI platform operated by Octathorn (Private) Limited, a company registered in Pakistan (Office No. 608, Alpha Techno Square (NASTP), Old Airport Building, Chaklala Cantt, Rawalpindi, Pakistan). Clawleaf provides AI-powered voice and messaging agents that help healthcare practices and other businesses ("Clients") communicate with their own customers and patients ("End Users"), including over the WhatsApp Business Platform, telephone, and web channels.

This policy applies to our websites, the Clawleaf platform, and the services we deliver on behalf of our Clients. For communications we handle on a Client's behalf, that Client is the controller of their End Users' personal data and Clawleaf acts as their service provider/processor; the Client's own privacy notice also applies.

2. Information We Collect

Information from Clients

  • Account and contact details (name, business name, email, phone number, role).
  • Business and billing information needed to set up and operate the service.
  • WhatsApp Business Platform data a Client authorizes us to access on their behalf, such as their WhatsApp Business Account ID, business phone numbers, phone number IDs, message templates, business profile details, and webhook/configuration settings.
  • Configuration you provide, such as agent scripts, workflows, business hours, and knowledge content.

Information about End Users (collected on behalf of our Clients)

  • Phone numbers and WhatsApp identifiers of people who contact, or are contacted by, a Client.
  • The content, metadata, audio, and transcripts of calls and messages handled by the AI agent (including appointment, scheduling, and service-related information an End User chooses to share).
  • Call and message status, timestamps, and interaction outcomes.

Information collected automatically

  • Log, device, and usage data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed) when you use our websites and platform.
  • Cookies and similar technologies used to operate and secure our websites. We choose privacy-preserving defaults and do not use non-essential tracking without consent where required.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Clawleaf platform and AI agents.
  • To send and receive messages and to place and receive calls over WhatsApp and other channels on behalf of our Clients.
  • To onboard and support Clients, including connecting and managing their WhatsApp Business Accounts, phone numbers, and message templates.
  • To generate transcripts, summaries, and dispositions so Clients can follow up with their customers and patients.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We only access and use Client and End User data to provide the Clawleaf service to that Client. We do not use the content of End User communications to train general-purpose models for unrelated purposes, and we do not use this data for advertising.

4. WhatsApp Business Platform and Meta

Clawleaf integrates with the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. When you or your Client use WhatsApp features, message and call data is transmitted through and processed by Meta in accordance with Meta's and WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policies. Our use of information received through the WhatsApp Business Platform complies with the applicable Meta Platform Terms, WhatsApp Business Terms, and Developer Policies. We access a Client's WhatsApp Business Account only after the Client explicitly authorizes us to do so, and only for the purposes of delivering the Clawleaf service to that Client.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal data. We share information only as described below:

  • With our Clients: we return call/message records, transcripts, and outcomes to the Client on whose behalf we handled the interaction.
  • With Meta/WhatsApp: to deliver messages and calls over the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • With service providers (sub-processors): trusted vendors that help us run the service, such as cloud hosting, storage, telephony, and speech/AI processing (speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and language-model providers), under contracts that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide services to us.
  • For legal and safety reasons: where required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of our services.
  • In a business transfer: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. Healthcare Information

Many of our Clients are healthcare practices, and the communications we process may include health-related information. Where we process such information on behalf of a healthcare Client, we act as that Client's service provider/processor and handle the information in accordance with our agreement with the Client and applicable healthcare privacy laws (for example, PHIPA in Ontario, Canada, or HIPAA in the United States, where applicable). We apply confidentiality and access controls appropriate to this information and process it only to provide the service.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as needed to provide the service, meet the retention periods configured by our Clients, and comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations, after which we delete or de-identify it. Clients may request deletion of their data and their End Users' data as described below.

8. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege access to Client accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and continuously improve our safeguards.

9. International Data Transfers

Clawleaf operates internationally, and your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including Pakistan and the countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object or withdraw consent. For data we process on behalf of a Client, please contact that Client (the controller); we will support their response to your request. You may also contact us directly using the details below and we will route your request appropriately.

11. Children's Privacy

Clawleaf is a business service and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children except where a healthcare Client lawfully processes a minor patient's information and instructs us to handle it on their behalf.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Continued use of our services after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
