Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Clawleaf, operated by Octathorn (Private) Limited, provides AI-powered voice and messaging agents to healthcare practices and other businesses ("Clients"), including over the WhatsApp Business Platform. This page explains how you can request the deletion of personal data that Clawleaf holds about you.

How to request deletion

To request that we delete your personal data, email us at hello@clawleaf.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • Your name;
  • The phone number, email, or account associated with the data; and
  • The business (Client) you interacted with, if known.

We will verify your request and, where we are able to act on it, delete the associated personal data we hold, and confirm once completed — normally within 30 days.

If you are a customer or patient of one of our Clients

When we handle communications on behalf of a business (for example, a healthcare practice), that business is the controller of your personal data and Clawleaf acts as its service provider/processor. You may contact that business directly to request deletion, or email us at hello@clawleaf.com and we will route your request to the relevant Client and support their response.

If you are a Client (business)

To delete your Clawleaf account and the data associated with it, email hello@clawleaf.com from your registered email address. Disconnecting your WhatsApp Business Account from Clawleaf also stops us from accessing that account's data going forward.

What we delete

On a valid request we delete the personal data we hold that is associated with the request, such as account details, message and call records, transcripts, and related metadata. We may retain limited information where required to comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations, after which it is deleted or de-identified.

Contact

For any questions about data deletion or your personal data, contact us at hello@clawleaf.com.
